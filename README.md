# Privacy Policy for Marble Spelling

**Effective Date:** Nov 18, 2026

## 1. Who We Are

We are the operator of the mobile app Marble Spelling (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information, with a special focus on protecting children’s privacy.

The App is intended for children, and this policy is written so that parents and guardians can easily understand it.

## 2. Zero Data Collection
**Marble Spelling does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.** 

* **No Personal Information:** We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, photos, location data, or device identifiers.
* **No Analytics or Tracking:** This app contains no third-party analytics SDKs, behavioral tracking, or profiling tools.
* **No Advertising:** This app is completely ad-free and does not connect to any advertising networks.
* **Offline Gameplay:** All game progress, audio pronunciation, and physics interactions happen locally on your device.

## 3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
Because we do not collect any data from any user, Marble Spelling fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), and Apple’s App Store Kids Category guidelines. We never knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 3. Third-Party Services
This app does not integrate with any third-party services, social media platforms, or external servers. All purchases are processed securely and exclusively by Apple through the App Store.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we ever update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated effective date.

## Quick Troubleshooting
* **No Sound?** Check that your iPhone or iPad is not on Silent Mode (check the side switch or Control Center) and that the media volume is turned up.
* **Marbles Not Rolling?** Make sure your device's motion sensors are working properly and try gently tilting or shaking the screen.
