1. Who We Are
We are the operator of the mobile app Marble Spelling (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information, with a special focus on protecting children’s privacy.
The App is intended for children, and this policy is written so that parents and guardians can easily understand it.

2. What This App Does Not Collect
The App does not collect personal data from children.
We do not ask for or collect:
- first name or last name,
- email address,
- phone number,
- date of birth,
- age,
- gender,
- school name,
- home address,
- location data,
- device identifiers,
- advertising identifiers,
- camera images,
- microphone recordings,
- photos,
- contacts,
- health data,
- financial data,
- payment card data,
- or any other information that can identify a child.
The App does not require an account, login, or registration.

3. What Information Stays on the Device
The App may store basic local information on the device so that the child’s experience works properly.
Examples of local information include:
- words the child has attempted,
- words the child has completed,
- words marked as mastered,
- hint usage,
- simple progress statistics,
- app settings,
- difficulty selection,
- sound, haptics, and motion preferences.
This information is stored only on the device where the App is installed.
We do not receive this local information.
We do not upload it.
We do not sync it to a cloud service.
We do not use it to identify the child.
You can remove this local information by using the App’s “Clear progress” option or by deleting the App from the device.

4. Children’s Privacy
The App is designed with children in mind.
We follow a simple rule: If we do not need the information, we do not collect it.
Because the App is designed for children, we do not:
-  ask children for personal information,
- ask children for parent or guardian information,
- require consent to collect personal data,
- show targeted advertising,
- sell data,
- share data with third parties,
- use third-party advertising networks,
- use third-party analytics,
- use tracking technologies,
- use cookies for tracking,
- use pixels or beacons,
- or contact children by email, phone, or message.

5. Ads, Analytics, and Tracking
The App does not use advertising.
The App does not use third-party analytics.
The App does not use tracking technologies.
The App does not include:
- ad SDKs,
- analytics SDKs,
- crash reporting services that collect personal data,
- attribution tools,
- marketing pixels,
- tracking cookies,
- device fingerprinting,
- advertising identifiers,
- or external analytics libraries.
If the App is submitted to Apple’s Kids Category, it is designed to comply with Apple’s Kids Category requirements by avoiding third-party ads, third-party analytics, and external links.

6. External Links
The App does not contain external links.
The App does not open:
- web browsers,
- websites,
- email apps,
- phone dialers,
- sharing sheets,
- maps,
- or other external apps.
This helps keep the experience safe and contained within the App.

7. Internet Access
The App is designed to work offline.
It does not require internet access for normal gameplay.
It does not send gameplay data, progress data, settings, or child information over the internet.

8. Motion and Device Sensors
If the optional “Tilt Jar” feature is enabled, the App may use basic device motion information to animate the marbles.
This motion information is used only on the device to control the visual behavior of the marbles.
It is not collected by us.
It is not sent to us.
It is not used to track the child’s location.
It is not used for advertising or analytics.
If device motion is unavailable or denied, the feature is simply disabled and the App remains fully playable using the Mix button.

9. Purchases
The App is sold as a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store.
If an App Store purchase is made, the transaction is handled through Apple’s standard App Store system. The App itself does not collect payment card information from children or parents.
We do not use in-app purchases for ads, subscriptions, or third-party payment processing.
If you later add in-app purchases, this policy should be reviewed and updated.

10. Third-Party Services
The App does not use third-party services that collect personal data from children.
We do not use:
- analytics providers,
- advertising providers,
- cloud services,
- social media platforms,
- email marketing tools,
- customer support chat services,
- crash reporting tools that collect user data,
- or data collection SDKs.
If we ever add a third-party service in the future, we will update this policy to explain what happens and why.

11. Data Sharing
We do not share personal data about children.
We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer personal data.
Because the App does not collect personal data from children, there is no personal child data to share.

12. Data Deletion and Parental Control
Because the App does not collect personal data from children, there is no personal child data for us to delete remotely.
Parents and guardians have simple control:
- Use the in-app Clear progress option to remove local progress.
- Delete the App from the device to remove all local App data.
- If you believe the App has retained any information you would like reviewed or removed, please contact us at: alohawaic@gmail.com
We will respond in a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law.

13. Security
The App is designed to minimize privacy risk by not collecting personal data.
Local information stored on the device is protected by the device’s own security features, such as passcodes, device encryption, and Apple’s standard privacy protections.
Because the App does not transmit personal data to external servers, there is no central database of child personal information to protect.

14. App Updates and Policy Changes
We may update the App over time to improve quality, accessibility, safety, or educational value.
If we make a material change to the App that affects privacy, we will update this Privacy Policy.
For example, if we ever add:
- accounts,
- cloud sync,
- child profiles with names,
- analytics,
- advertising,
- external links,
- social features,
- in-app purchases,
- or any feature that collects personal data,
we will update this policy before releasing that feature.
Changes will be reflected by updating the “Effective Date” at the top of this policy.
